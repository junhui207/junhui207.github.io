⚔️ ARMY COMMANDER: INFINITE CONQUEST

Click soldiers to select them. Shift-click to add soldiers. Drag to select groups.
Right-click the ground to move selected soldiers. Capture bases to reveal more regions.
I: Army invincibility for 60 seconds, costs 1 gold.
K: Kill all enemy troops in the current region, earn their gold, preserve bases. Free, 60-second cooldown.
